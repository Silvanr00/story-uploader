Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the tool accesses

Story Uploader asks Google for two permissions on the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's own videos as private videos.
  • Read channel information (youtube.readonly): only to confirm which channel is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • The access token stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The tool does not collect, store or process data of any other person.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The tool has no website login, no analytics and no cookies.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account under myaccount.google.com/connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Story Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
