Story Uploader

Story Uploader is a small private tool. It uploads the owner's own animated short stories to the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • Kinda Round Fr
  • Lil Blocky Feels

Every video is made by the owner. The tool only uploads it as a private video. The owner reviews each video in YouTube Studio and decides personally whether to publish it.

The tool is not offered to anyone else. Nobody can sign up for it.
